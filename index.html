$Open Bounties

Get paid for fixing open source.

Maintainers post bounties on real issues and features. Contributors claim them, ship the work, and earn. Payouts are settled by the bounty owner — this board tracks everything publicly.

Activity

    ⚠️ Payouts are internal accounting only. This board never moves real money. When work is marked complete, the payout is flagged “pending owner approval” and the bounty poster settles it off-platform.
